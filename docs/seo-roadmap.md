# SEO / GEO Roadmap (post-launch)

_Advanced SEO ideas beyond the V1 baseline that ships with the admin build
(see [admin-design.md §8.1](admin-design.md) — sitemap, robots, JSON-LD,
llms.txt, meta/OG). These are post-launch levers, roughly in order of
bang-for-buck. Renamed from `SEO.md` 2026-07-22; original owner question:
"is there any more advanced way to do SEO?"_

1. One page per thing people search for (biggest lever)
Right now you have essentially one indexable page competing for every search. Advanced e-commerce SEO inverts that: a dedicated URL for each product (/products/eternal-red-rose) and for each occasion people actually type into Google — "anniversary gift for wife", "preserved rose vs real rose", "Valentine's day gold rose". Each page targets one search intent deeply. Done systematically (dozens of occasion/audience pages generated from a template + data), this is called programmatic SEO — it's how big stores dominate search. For you, even 5–10 hand-made pages would multiply your surface area.

2. Google Merchant Center (free product listings)
This one is e-commerce specific and underused: you feed Google your product catalogue (name, price, photo, stock) and your products can appear in the Shopping tab and product carousels for free — separate from normal ranking, and much faster to show results. Your structured data is already halfway there. This is arguably the highest bang-for-buck "advanced" move for a small store.

3. Rich results: reviews and ratings
Your product schema can carry star ratings and review counts, which show up as gold stars directly in Google results — dramatically higher click rates. The catch: you need real collected reviews (fake ones get penalized). So this becomes available once you have actual customers, and it's worth wiring up early.

4. Content that earns authority
Google ranks sites it considers authoritative, and authority comes from other websites linking to you. You can't code that — you earn it with content worth linking to: "how 24K gold dipping works" with your process photos, gift guides journalists might cite, comparison pages. This is the slow, compounding part of SEO — and the part competitors can't copy overnight.

5. The new frontier: being the answer in AI search
Increasingly people ask ChatGPT/Google AI "best preserved rose gift" instead of scrolling results. Getting cited there (sometimes called GEO/AEO) rewards the same things: clear factual pages, strong structured data, being mentioned on other reputable sites. Your structured-data head start genuinely helps here.

And one un-glamorous prerequisite
Google Search Console — a free Google dashboard that shows which searches you appear for, your click rates, and indexing problems. Nothing above is worth doing blind; this is the instrument panel, takes ~10 minutes to connect, and I'd genuinely do it before any of the rest.

The realistic sequence for you: Search Console now → the small fixes from earlier → product/occasion pages when real products firm up → Merchant Center → reviews once orders flow. Happy to start on any of these — Search Console setup plus the earlier three small fixes would be a natural first bite.
