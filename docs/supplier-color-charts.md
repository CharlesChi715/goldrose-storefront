## Transcription flags (as printed on the charts — confirm with supplier)

- Y chart: the first rose is labeled **"YS-1 Red"** — almost certainly a typo
  for **Y-1** (recorded as Y-1 above).
- Y chart header repeats the red subtitle *"Gold Plated Series with Star
  Glitter"* even though its names are plain solids with no glitter suffix —
  likely a copy-paste of the YS chart header; treated here as the classic
  series.
- **YC-16 and YC-17 are both printed "Multicolor Bloom"** (different flowers);
  **YC-1 and YC-36 are both printed "Lotus Violet Edge"**. Codes stay unique,
  so use codes — not names — as identifiers.
- YC-22 is printed **"VelvGolden Violet Ombre"** — probably meant
  "Velvet Golden Violet Ombre".
- Names use inconsistent capitalization on the charts (e.g. "Y-8 snow-white",
  "Y-11 light green"); normalized to Title Case above.
