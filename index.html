Questions

Remove buyer hesitation.

Is checkout connected?

Not yet. This is the storefront prototype. The next step is connecting Stripe Checkout, Shopify Buy Button, or another payment flow.

Can the product details change?

Yes. Product names, images, descriptions, and prices are stored in script.js so they are easy to edit while learning.

Why not start with a big framework?

Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript make the fundamentals visible. Once the store concept is clear, moving to Next.js, Shopify, or Medusa is much easier.
